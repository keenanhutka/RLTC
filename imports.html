Brand no. 1

Arcana Artis

Handcrafted ritual candles made by Alisa La Volpe Scura in Florence, Italy.

Alisa makes every candle by hand. Her range includes rose candles, moon candles, a corn candle for Lammas, earthen candles cast with herbs and roots, and three-candle sets for love, abundance and inspiration.

Rabbit Lake Trading Company imports Arcana Artis to Canada and offers it to Canadian retailers. The full collection, with photos and descriptions, lives on the brand's own site.
