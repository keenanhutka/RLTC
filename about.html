About

A trading post for the north

Rabbit Lake Trading Company brings imports, handmade pieces and local craft together under one roof.

Who we are

Northern goods, chosen with care

Rabbit Lake Trading Company is run by Keenan Hutka in the Kenora area of Northwestern Ontario. It's an import and distribution company, and also a home for handmade work, both our own and that of local makers.

The idea is simple: find things that are made well and by hand, and get them into the hands of shops and people who will appreciate them.

[A few lines from Keenan: your art, what drew you to start RLTC, and the story behind the name.]

What we do

Three parts of the business

  1. Import and distribute

    We bring handcrafted goods to Canada and offer them to retailers. Arcana Artis candles from Florence are the first.

  2. Make by hand

    Small jewellery, costume supplies, and pieces from our art, including swords and outfits. These are on the way.

  3. Carry local work

    We want to help local craftspeople reach more people by carrying their work alongside ours.

Our approach

Honest goods, plainly described

What you see is what we sell. Product photos on this site are real photos from the makers; illustrations are marked as illustrations. If you have a question about anything we carry, just ask.
